What I Do

I build software that thinks — AI systems, data pipelines, and the tools that connect them.

From structured document engines to intelligent agents, I work at the boundary between what machines can parse and what humans actually mean.

Philosophy

Most problems aren't technical. They're structural — the wrong abstraction, the wrong boundary, the wrong question asked too late.

I believe in building small, understanding deeply, and shipping things that survive contact with reality.
